# SAKU — Master Plan (Updated with Branding & Identity)

*AI dream-to-action planner buat otak ADHD & siapa aja yang mentok mau ngapain. Dibangun solo pake Claude Code. Positioning: wellness/productivity, BUKAN alat medis.*

-----

## TL;DR

- Rebrand dari “Momentum” jadi **SAKU** (internal “Saku Radio”, publik cukup “Saku” = “pocket” di Bahasa Indonesia/Melayu). Dual-layer: publik = app ramah kantong di saku lo; hidden = “radio” itu radio BUTTON (pilih satu opsi doang), bukan radio musik. Radio button = filosofi produk: satu plan aktif, step-by-step, no multitasking — align banget sama best practice ADHD (single-tasking) dan free tier (1 plan).
- Brand identity dibangun di atas radio button sebagai design language: logomark camouflaged (counter huruf “a”/“u” jadi lingkaran-dengan-titik), progress indicator = rantai radio dot vertikal, task completion = dot fill + haptic. Zero ikonografi radio musik. Palet calm base + dopamine accent, font rounded geometric.
- Trademark: “Saku” = kata generik (“pocket”), padat banget di fintech Indonesia (Sakuku BCA, i.saku Indomaret) TAPI zero konflik di kategori planner/produktivitas/ADHD. Risiko likelihood-of-confusion di Class 9/42 planner = rendah, tapi discoverability di app store susah. Wajib cek manual PDKI Class 9 & 42.

## Key Findings

1. **Nama SAKU works di dua level.** “Saku” gampang diucapin global, rooted Indonesia, connote “ramah kantong” (free tier). Radio button metaphor secara ilmiah nyambung: riset ADHD konsisten bilang multitasking itu mitos buat otak ADHD, single-tasking lebih produktif & less anxiety. Jadi “cuma bisa pilih satu, kayak radio button, bukan checkbox” itu bukan gimmick — itu evidence-based.
1. **Radio button = design language yang bisa di-camouflage.** Kayak FedEx arrow: hidden bonus di negative space. Per FedEx logo designer Lindon Leader (Landor Associates, dikutip CNN/The Mirror): “The prevailing notion is – I’ve heard – that perhaps less than one in five people find the hidden arrow unaided.” Design-savvy orang notice “eh ini radio button!”, orang biasa liat app biasa. Insider easter egg.
1. **Trademark: generik tapi aman di kategori.** Gak ada app “Saku” yang planner/ADHD. Incumbent semua fintech. saku.app parked/for-sale, trysaku.com taken.
1. **Corporate story built-in.** PT Sakuradio Development Technology (SAKUDEV) = company literally namanya Sakuradio. Founding story kuat.

## Details

### 1. Nama & Filosofi (Dual-Layer)

**Public layer — “Saku” = pocket.** Mobile app yang hidup di saku lo. Connote “ramah kantong”: affordable, forever free tier. Simple, pendek, memorable, Indonesian-rooted tapi globally pronounceable.

**Hidden layer — “Radio” = RADIO BUTTON, bukan radio musik.** Radio button cuma boleh pilih SATU opsi at a time — beda sama checkbox yang boleh multi-select (multitasking). Ini encode core product philosophy: satu plan aktif, step-by-step, no multitasking. Align sama ADHD best practice (single-tasking) & free tier (1 plan). Metaphor: “lo cuma bisa nyelesain opsi satu-satu, step by step, kayak radio button, bukan kayak checkbox.”

**CONSTRAINT KRITIS: radio harus FULLY CAMOUFLAGED.** ZERO ikonografi radio musik — no antena, no radio wave, no menara siaran, no radio jadul, no sound wave. Radio concept manifest CUMA lewat radio button UI shape: lingkaran dengan titik terisi pas selected. Orang liat “Saku” = app ramah kantong; cuma design-savvy yang lama-lama notice “oh logonya radio button!”. Insider design easter egg.

Ini ilmiah: riset UX konsisten. Nielsen Norman Group (Jakob Nielsen, “Checkboxes vs. Radio Buttons,” 2004): radio buttons “restrict them to selecting exactly one option,” sementara checkboxes bikin user bisa “select any number of choices, including zero, one, or several.” NN/G (“Checkboxes: Design Guidelines”): “Radio buttons are mutually exclusive, and one and only one must always be selected… Never use circles as checkboxes.” Aturan checkbox vs radio button udah sama sejak Inside Macintosh 1984 — NN/G nulis “Even after twenty years, it’s worth stating these guidelines once again.”

### 2. Logo Direction

Prinsip hidden-meaning logo (FedEx arrow, Baskin Robbins 31, Continental tire): meaning dibangun ke dalam typography/negative space, bukan ditempel sebagai simbol terpisah. Tim Lindon Leader bikin “over 400 versions of the logo” sebelum nemu arrow di antara E/x (wordmark-nya blend Univers 67 Bold Condensed + Futura Bold); arrow-nya “discovered, not invented” dan tetep reinforce brand values di subconscious walau minoritas yang notice. Pelajaran: jangan force icon, biarin letterform lo yang guide solusi.

**Konsep logomark SAKU (camouflaged radio button):**

- **Konsep A — counter jadi radio button.** Counter huruf “a” atau “u” di “SAKU” di-render sebagai lingkaran sempurna dengan titik terisi di tengah = radio button selected state. Wordmark keliatan normal; radio button ngumpet di dalem huruf.
- **Konsep B — app icon = single radio button selected.** Satu lingkaran dengan dot terisi di tengah, di atas background solid/gradient. Simpel, geometric, recognizable di 60px. Notion “N”, Linear geometric shape, Arc minimal arc = benchmark app icon minimalis yang works di segala ukuran.
- **Konsep C — “S” mark.** Huruf S yang terminusnya jadi dot radio button.

**Compliance app icon (Apple/Google):**

- Apple: master 1024×1024 px, PNG, square (sistem yang kasih rounded/squircle mask ~22% corner radius), no transparency/alpha (transparan = render item black), harus recognizable di 29×29 px. Support light/dark/tinted (iOS 18+ liquid glass), pake Icon Composer. Untuk dark/tinted, keluarin background, pake grayscale.
- Google Play: adaptive icon (foreground + background layer), master 512×512 px PNG <1MB, no shadow/corner (Google yang nambahin), safe zone inner ~66%, plus monochrome layer buat Android 13+ themed icons.
- Single radio button dot = perfect buat ini: satu shape, dua warna, kebaca di ukuran terkecil. A/B test via Apple Product Page Optimization (up to 3 varian) & Google Play Store Listing Experiments, minimal 7 hari.

### 3. Visual Identity — Palet Warna

Konteks neurodivergent design: base harus low-sensory (soft, muted, calm — gentle blues/greens/teal, warm neutral), HINDARI kontras ekstrem & warna over-saturated yang jarring. TAPI ADHD brains juga butuh stimulation & dopamine hit — jadi accent bright dipake HEMAT buat celebration/reward moment (task done, plan complete). Riset color-coding: pake shade yang cukup kontras buat WCAG tapi jangan andelin warna doang (kombinasi sama ikon + teks). Provide “Calm Mode” (dim warna, stop animasi) — best practice neurodivergent.

Gen Z appeal 2025: dopamine color (bright pink, electric blue, sunny yellow, vibrant green) lagi naik, TAPI prediksi late-2026 bakal ada “dopamine fatigue”. Strategi Saku: **calm base + strategic dopamine accent** — dapet dua-duanya tanpa overwhelm.

**Palet usulan (hex):**

- **Base / Surface:** `#F7F6F2` (warm off-white/paper), `#FFFFFF` surface, dark mode `#14161A`.
- **Primary (calm anchor):** Teal-ish `#3BA99C` (calming, “medical scrub” association tanpa keliatan medis, aman buat blue/yellow recognition ADHD & color-blind).
- **Ink / Text:** `#1F2933` (bukan pure black — kurangi harsh contrast).
- **Dopamine accent (celebration only):** Coral/tangerine `#FF7A59` + optional sunny `#FFC542`. Dipake pas dot fill / plan complete.
- **Muted secondary:** Sage `#A9C5A0`, soft lavender `#B8B3E9`.
  Colors sparingly, banyak white/neutral space = sense of calm.

### 4. Tipografi

Butuh rounded geometric sans yang echo bentuk lingkaran (radio button DNA). Opsi gratis di Google Fonts (aman buat mobile app, bisa di-embed):

- **Poppins** — geometric sans, lingkaran hampir sempurna, populer, echo bentuk dot. Bagus buat display/headline.
- **DM Sans** — low-contrast geometric, variable font, di-design khusus buat text size kecil (bagus UI body). “Calm” feeling.
- **Varela Round / M PLUS Rounded 1c / Quicksand** — rounded terminal, friendly, echo circle. M PLUS Rounded 1c juga cover Jepang kalau perlu.
- **Nunito / Rubik** — rounded corner, ramah, kebaca.
  Rekomendasi: **Display = Poppins (atau Quicksand buat lebih rounded), Body/UI = DM Sans** (variable, optimized small size). Catatan: geometric font shape berulang bisa nurunin readability di body panjang — makanya body pake DM Sans yang lebih optimized, bukan Poppins.

### 5. Motion Design Language

Signature micro-interaction = **radio-button dot animation**: dot fill (kosong → keisi pas task selesai), dot traveling antar circle (step 1 → step 2). Ini jadi tanda tangan Saku.

- Task completion: dot fills + haptic yang satisfying (multisensory reinforcement — visual + tactile bikin feeling of control & achievement, kayak confetti tapi subtle). Sync animasi + haptic ≤50ms biar gak keliatan sluggish.
- Prinsip: setiap microinteraction harus punya purpose; jangan overload (ADHD-sensitive — animasi berlebih bisa distracting). Consistent style. Lightweight biar gak makan baterai.
- Implementasi: `react-native-reanimated` (worklet, 60fps di UI thread) buat animasi dot; `expo-haptics` buat haptic. Success haptic = `Haptics.notificationAsync(NotificationFeedbackType.Success)`, selection = `Haptics.selectionAsync()`, impact = `Haptics.impactAsync(ImpactFeedbackStyle.Light/Medium)`. Bikin `useHaptic()` hook reusable. Note: iOS Taptic mati kalau Low Power Mode, dictation, atau camera aktif — jadi haptic HARUS complement visual, jangan gantiin.

### 6. UI Design Language — Radio Button Sistemik

Radio button shape jadi elemen desain di seluruh app:

- **Progress indicator = rantai radio dot vertikal.** Step 1 filled (done) → step 2 active (pulsing) → step 3 empty (belum). Visual literal dari “one at a time”.
- **Plan selection = literal radio group.** Cuma bisa pilih SATU active plan (free tier). Radio button = default selection selalu ada satu (NN/G rule: “one and only one must always be selected”).
- **Task completion animation** = dot fill + haptic.
- **Empty state / onboarding** pake radio dot metaphor buat ngajarin filosofi “pilih satu, selesaikan”.
- **Checkbox = anti-pattern yang brand-nya reject.** Bisa dipake di marketing: “checkbox apps overwhelm you; Saku is a radio button.” Checkbox = multi-select = multitasking = overwhelm; radio = single = focus.

Component library: bisa pake pendekatan shadcn-style design token buat Expo (contoh: BNA UI — Expo RN components pake design token + reanimated + expo-haptics). Semua style reference theme token (`useTheme()` → colors, spacing), NO hardcoded value, biar konsisten & gampang dark mode.

### 9. Marketing / Positioning Update

**Brand story = radio button philosophy.** Hook: “Checkbox apps let you multitask into overwhelm; Saku only lets you pick one.” Ini differentiator visual + filosofis sekaligus. Founding story: company literally namanya PT Sakuradio Development Technology — brand tie back ke company name.

**Positioning vs Tiimo/Numo:** Tiimo = sensory-friendly visual planner/scheduling (kuat di sensory design, visual timer). Numo = ADHD gamified. Saku beda: **AI dream-to-action** — capture angan conversationally, AI simplify jadi smallest next action, + agentic AI research layer (contoh: trip Jepang → AI riset visa, budget, itinerary). Plus free tier genuinely free (1 plan aktif sampe selesai, unlimited sequential).

**App Store listing / ASO:**

- Nama app harus brand + descriptor (karena “Saku” generik). Format: `Brand: keyword + keyword` atau `keyword – Brand`. Title iOS max 30 char, Google Play max 50 char. Title = ranking factor terkuat.
- Rekomendasi title: **“Saku: AI Planner for ADHD”** (26 char) atau **“Saku: ADHD Planner & To-Do”**. Subtitle iOS (30 char): “One task at a time, done” atau “Dream to action, step by step”.
- iOS keyword field (100 char, invisible): adhd,planner,focus,task,todo,goal,productivity,executive,function,dopamine,plan.
- Per AppTweak study 2025 (top 100 free apps di BR/FR/DE/IN/ES/UK/US): rata-rata 41% app di App Store dan 47% app di Google Play masukin keyword generik di title selain brand. Per Apple Ads: “Search is the primary way people discover apps on the App Store — 70% of App Store visitors use search to discover apps,” dan “Almost 65% of downloads happen directly after a search.” Riset native keyword per bahasa (jangan translate literal — case study: translate literal bikin -43% search visibility di pasar Jepang).
- Category: Productivity atau Health & Fitness (wellness). BUKAN Medical.

## Recommendations

**Tahap 0 (sekarang, sebelum MVP build) — Brand foundation, ~1 minggu:**

1. Lock nama SAKU. Cek manual PDKI (pdki-indonesia.dgip.go.id) Class 9 & 42 buat “SAKU” + “SAKURADIO”; kalau ada budget, konsultan KI buat clearance. Threshold: kalau ada merek “Saku” TERDAFTAR di Class 9/42 buat software produktivitas/planner → pertimbang varian (Saku app, SakuOne, dsb) atau file cepet (first-to-file).
1. Amankan domain + social handle. Beli `sakuradio.com` (aman, tie company) + cek/beli `getsaku.com`/`saku.id`. Grab `@sakuapp`/`@heysaku` di IG/TikTok/X.
1. Bikin logo: 3 konsep (counter-a radio button, single-dot app icon, S-mark). Test recognizability di 29px & 60px. Pilih yang lolos Apple/Google guideline.
1. Define design token: colors (palet di atas), typography (Poppins + DM Sans), spacing, radius. Taruh di theme file Expo (`useTheme()`).

**Tahap 1–5 (roadmap ~20 minggu, existing 5 fase):**

- **Phase 0 (baru/expand): Brand + Design System** — logo, app icon (light/dark/tinted + adaptive), design token, `<RadioDot>` component (empty/active/filled state + fill animation via reanimated), `useHaptic()` hook, `<ProgressChain>` (rantai radio dot vertikal). Deliverable: Figma/token + core component library.
- **Phase 1: MVP core** — auth (Clerk), 1 active plan (radio group), conversational capture → AI simplify (Haiku 4.5), task list pake RadioDot + completion animation + haptic. Tech: Expo + TS, Hono/Next API, Prisma + Neon PostgreSQL.
- **Phase 2: Agentic research layer** — AI riset online (Sonnet 4.6 + web search) buat plan (visa/budget/itinerary). Hybrid routing Haiku/Sonnet, target cost $0.45–0.75/active user/bulan.
- **Phase 3: Monetization** — RevenueCat, chips (consumable IAP) buat unlock PARALLEL plan (3/5/max 10). Free tier tetap: 1 plan aktif genuinely free sampe selesai, unlimited sequential.
- **Phase 4: Polish + ASO** — App Store listing (title/subtitle/keyword di atas), screenshot annotated (hook “checkbox overwhelm, Saku pilih satu”), A/B test icon (PPO/SLE), Calm Mode, onboarding radio-dot.
- **Phase 5: Launch + iterate** — soft launch SEA (Indonesian-English), monitor keyword ranking, retention, cost/user. Iterate.

**Benchmark yang ngubah keputusan:**

- Kalau cost AI/user > $0.75/bulan → tighten hybrid routing (lebih banyak Haiku, cache research).
- Kalau ada konflik trademark Class 9/42 kategori sama → rebrand varian sebelum spend marketing.
- Kalau A/B icon: butuh 10k+ impression/varian buat signifikan; kalau conversion naik → keep.
